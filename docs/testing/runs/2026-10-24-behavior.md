## Defect detection

Two temporary implementation mutations were tested together against the focused
Chromium contracts. Authentication passed; both targeted cases failed at their
intended assertions:

1. In `PoiMarkers`, replacing `return folder.visible` with `return true` made the
   hidden-POI-folder test receive a marker where it expected none.
2. In `applyLanderLocationUpdateStage`, retaining the old lander-copy station
   location made the relocation test reject that station's coordinates.

Both source files were restored byte-for-byte before the final run. The mutation
report is retained under `.local/playwright/reports/mutations/`; its log is
`.local/playwright-mutations.log`. Expected mutant failures are not counted as
failures of the restored application.
